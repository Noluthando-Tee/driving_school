Our Mission

To provide Affordable, high-quality K53 driving training (code 8, 10, & 14) that empowers every leader from Durban to rural KZN to obtain their license first time, with real-world driving skills that save lives, not just pass tests.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and leading driving school in Durban, known for producing safe, confident, and responsible drivers who respect the road.


Our Objective

FOR OUR LEARNERS: To achieve a 100% first-time pass rate through proper k53 method. To make every Learners confident in hill-start, parallel parking, and highway driving. To offer patient, Zulu & English speaking instructors. FOR BUSINESS: To provide Affordable packages with flexible pay-as-you-go option. To offer door-to-door pick-up in and around Durban, Umlazi, Adams, Mbumbulu. To maintain clean, roadworthy, dual-control training vihicles. FOR SAFETY: To teach defensive driving and road safety, not just test routes. To produce zero-accident drivers To educate about zero tolerance for drunk driving

ABOUT US

Owned by Sisters. Built for the Community.Shamase Driving School is a proud 100% family-owned business, founded in March 2026 by two sisters, Thando Mkhwanazi and Zama Mkhwanazi.As young women from KZN, we know how hard it is to get your licence — the fear, the expensive lessons, the impatient instructors. We started Shamase because we wanted to create the driving school we wished we had: patient, affordable, and run with heart.The name SHAMASE honours our family and our roots. It stands for respect, responsibility, and moving forward together.We are not a big, cold company. When you call 062 653 8701, you speak directly to Thando or Zama. We are the owners, the admins, and the instructors who will be with you from your first learners class to the day you pass your test. Our Promise to You: We don't just teach you to pass the test — we teach you to drive so you can get a job, take your kids to school, and feel safe on the road for life. Every learner is family to us.Why Choose Two Sisters?More Patient: We specialize in nervous first-time driversLadies-Friendly: Many women in Durban feel safer learning with female instructorsWe Understand the Struggle: Flexible payments, door-to-door pickup, Zulu & English lessonsWe Are Always Available: Mon-Sat 07:00-17:00From our family to yours — thank you for trusting us to be part of your journey.
